Zero-Trust Security & Privacy Policy

✓ No Biometric Storage in Browser: Raw webcam frames and biometric embedding representations are never stored in localStorage, cookies, or IndexedDB.

✓ Server-Side Cryptography: Reference facial vectors are encrypted server-side with AES-256-GCM using isolated tenant keys and salted initialization vectors.

✓ Camera Stream Termination: MediaTracks are strictly released upon page unload or manual pause to prevent unintended background surveillance.
